Schemas 0.1.12. A free object's number may give its unit (`x-unit`: `m`, `s`, `×`, or `%`, which shows a fraction as a percentage), a vector may name its numbers as a size (`x-axes: "size"`), and a `range` may go without a scale: it then shows as two fields, Min and Max. A number on a short scale is a slider by default; `x-control: "number"` keeps a field.
