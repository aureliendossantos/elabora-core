Branching dialogues (D61), the format, the walk and the tools: a built-in `dialogue` block holds its conversation — nodes of lines (a narration line's fields with an expression, cues on words and a condition), options always said by the learner with a quality (good, ok, bad), a reply, a trainer's note, points, effects on the dialogue's own variables, retry, once and fallback, and endings chosen by conditions. An option without `next` joins its node's way out. One pure walk in `@elabora/player` (`dialogue/`) is replayed from the list of choices for resume, going back, new versions and « Jouer un chemin »; the first run to an end is the only one scored, first choices counting (quality, points over the highest sum the graph allows, or the ending's score), with one choice interaction per decision. Modes (practice, assessment, exploration) set going back, feedback and scoring. Speakers take a `color`, a `look` (an SVG or pictures by expression) and `narrator`. Events gain the triggers `dialogue.started`, `.node`, `.line`, `.chosen` and `.ended`, conditions on a dialogue's scored run and the actions `start-dialogue`, `goto-dialogue` and `restart-dialogue`; options and cues run presentation actions only. New commands `elabora dialogue show | play | paths | export` (the client's page: the map, laid out by the core, then the script, as HTML or PDF) and agent operations `elabora_dialogue_show`, `_play`, `_paths`, `_export`, `elabora_set_dialogue_node` and `elabora_remove_dialogue_node`; engine requests for the app's editor. Rise scenarios import as dialogues. New codes: E_DIALOGUE_TARGET, E_DIALOGUE_DUPLICATE_ID, E_DIALOGUE_DEAD_END, E_DIALOGUE_SPEAKER, E_DIALOGUE_CONDITION, E_DIALOGUE_VAR, E_DIALOGUE_ACTION, E_DIALOGUE_CUE, W_DIALOGUE_UNREACHABLE, W_DIALOGUE_LOOP, W_DIALOGUE_OPTIONS_MANY, W_DIALOGUE_OPTIONS_UNEVEN, W_DIALOGUE_LINE_LONG, W_DIALOGUE_SCORE_MISSING, W_DIALOGUE_ENDINGS_UNCOVERED, W_DIALOGUE_TREE, W_DIALOGUE_NO_RETURN, W_DIALOGUE_LOOK, W_DIALOGUE_DECISION_CAP. The "Dialogues" page, the `elabora-dialogues` skill and the course rules (revision 12) guide agents. The player's rendering of dialogues comes next.
