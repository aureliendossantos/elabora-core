Free-object manifests take three more editor controls: `range` (a low and a high number on one scale, an array of two numbers whose items have a minimum and a maximum, shown as a slider with two handles), `vec2` and `vec3` (two or three numbers, shown as X, Y and Z on one row). `W_OBJECT_PROP_CONTROL` checks them: the count of numbers, the range's scale, and a default whose low value comes first. The contract, the free-objects skill and the components guide say when to use them.
