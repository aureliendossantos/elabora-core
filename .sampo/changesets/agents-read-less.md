What agents read and look at costs less. `elabora object map . <id>` (MCP `elabora_object_map`) gives a free object's code by sections, with their lines and functions, its props, words, objects, state, events and tests (`--tests`: each test's steps on one line), in a few thousand characters instead of the whole object. `elabora docs --search <words>` (`elabora_docs { search }`) finds the sections of the pages, decisions and skills that hold them. An option a command does not know, or a value missing, prints that command's usage, not its whole group; an unknown command lists the commands and the near ones. `elabora look` says each control of a screen's free objects that the page covers (a bar, a pane: a click there would not reach it), and `--controls` lists every control with its box in the window, as text. A look or a screenshot at a moment says that free objects are not on the screen's timeline, and points at `elabora object replay`. The course rules (revision 15) and `elabora-course-authoring` say that an adjustment is the change asked for and only it: the extras are proposed, an open visual choice is one question.
