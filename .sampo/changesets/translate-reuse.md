`elabora translate reuse [dir] --to <lang> --from <course or .xlf>` fills the units still to translate (or stale) whose source text another course already translated, as one undoable edit; units with marks, over their maximum length, or whose maths, links, code, blanks or placeholders would change are left to translate.
