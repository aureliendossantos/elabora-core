The editor canvas reports the texts of a screen outside its blocks as screen readers meet them (`texts` of `elabora/layout`): the screen's title (also when it is not shown), the texts its layout marks, its reading time and pictures, and an open layer's title, each with where it is, the first block after it in the page and whether screen readers read it (not under `aria-hidden`, a picture with its alternative).
