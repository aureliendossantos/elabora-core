Course rules (AGENTS.md) and the authoring guidance scale the checks to what a change does:
- **A presentation-only fix** (a duration, an easing, a colour, a decoration that moves nothing) leaves the plan alone and needs one `elabora look` of the affected screen, in the state that shows the issue. It needs no fit and no object gates.
- **A layout change** adds `elabora fit` on the screens it reaches.
- **Content, structure or behaviour** take the whole route.

Agents broaden the checks only when one of them reveals a problem.
