`elabora look --block <id> --act …` drives a free object that carries on from another screen (continuity, D65): its frame, in the page's keep layer, is found through the block's `aria-owns`.

Validation and the narration's timeline carry on past a narration line whose text is not a string (a screen that breaks its schema): the line reads as empty, and the schema error says why.

The repository's sample `planetary-gearset` is replaced by `examples/clockwork`, « Why is the hour hand twelve times slower? », in English with a French translation: one 3D object carries its seven screens, from the clock face to the gear train and back to the dial seen through, with the controls of each screen only. The old sample stays as a test fixture of the engine.
