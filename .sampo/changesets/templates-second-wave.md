Three more module templates (D68): Editorial (« Revue »: the magazine page, warm paper, ink rules and folios, a colour plate for each part, texts that come in through masks), Paper (« Atelier »: cut paper on kraft with real soft shadows, a paper theatre that moves in stop motion) and Watercolour (« Aquarelle »: washes of water tones that spread from one screen to the next). Like the four before them, they have the 14 common screens, a French translation and two or three backdrop variants; `elabora templates` lists them and `elabora init --template` makes a course from them.
