No coloured rule down the side of the player's boxes any more, a mark of generic interfaces. A callout is a tinted card held by a hairline of its tone (`--t-callout-tone`: primary, accent for important, error for warning, success for tip); the expert answer of a reflection is a card of the same kind; the prompt of cards, tabs and accordions is an italic line; a note in the margin is smaller, quieter text; the UI kit's `.t-ui-card` of free objects is a tinted card. `examples/planetary-gearset` follows.
