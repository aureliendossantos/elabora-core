`elabora look --block <dialogue> --act choose:a --act choose:c` (MCP `elabora_look` with `block` and `act`) shows a dialogue block at rest after those choices, as the app's editor shows a path: a close-up with the end of its thread in the window (the trainer's notes, the next options, or the debrief once it has ended), and the node waiting, the ending and the score. A choice not offered is refused with the options that were. The sample courses diagnostic-reseau and phishing-by-numbers now show voiced dialogues: a messaging call with a layer that speaks, and a scene with a new caller, a backdrop and an email panel the call opens.
