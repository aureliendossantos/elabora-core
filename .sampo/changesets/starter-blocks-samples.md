A new sample, coffee-in-brief, is a Rise-style scroll course for the baristas of a fictional café chain, from cherry to cup in seven pages: it uses every starter block and each of its presentations, the opening layout in its four, steps that wait for exploration or for an answer, an optional block, a character, pre-questions and a scored check, with pictures drawn by its own generator. In medieval-charts the scrolling workshop and the figure in layers are forks of « Étapes » (any number of stages and layers, no longer six and eight), the chart with notes a fork of « Image interactive », and its glosses and diptych built-in texts with an aside and columns styled by the theme.
