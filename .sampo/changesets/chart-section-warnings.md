Two warnings (D92). `W_CHART_ITEM_BEFORE_BLOCK`: the voice brings a chart's item before the chart appears (its `timing.in`, or its step), so learners never see that item come. `W_SCREEN_OUTSIDE_PART`: a screen covers an item of another part of the plan than its section's, so the section no longer follows its part when the plan's parts move. Neither refuses a change nor blocks a release.
