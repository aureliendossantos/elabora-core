A course's translation now covers the words authors read in the editor about its design: what its styles and backdrop variants are for, its components' names, descriptions, slots and settings, and the settings and events of the free objects its blocks place. They are written into their own files in the translated course, so a module made in French from a template reads in French in the Charte and the inspector too. They are editor words: never shown to learners, « Rechercher » does not find them, and they hold no language back: `translate status` counts them apart (`editor`), and a translation without them is still ready, the translated course keeping the source's words there. The seven templates and orbital-rendezvous carry their French.
