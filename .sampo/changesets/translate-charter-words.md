A course's translation now covers the words authors read in the editor about its design: what its styles and backdrop variants are for, its components' names, descriptions, slots and settings, and the settings and events of the free objects its blocks place. They are written into their own files in the translated course, so a module made in French from a template reads in French in the Charte and the inspector too. They are editor words: never shown to learners, and « Rechercher » does not find them. The seven templates and orbital-rendezvous carry their French.
