A session syncing a project in the cloud says who is there: `cloudStatus` events carry the copies connected (`peers`, each with its person or agent) and this copy's id (`client`), and `session.cloud` gives them with the team and project. `session.linkCloud(options)` starts syncing a folder linked while its session is open.
