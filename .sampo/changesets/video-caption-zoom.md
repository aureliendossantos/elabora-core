Schemas 0.1.11: a video is an illustration as an image is. A video block takes an image's `caption`, shown under it as an image's (under a video played with its controls, in place of its title, which still names it); a loop takes `zoom`: the video is the button that opens it at full size in the lightbox, with its caption, where it loops too, with its own pause button. `elabora media to-video` keeps an image's caption and zoom; the video's title, which names it in the editor, is its alt.
