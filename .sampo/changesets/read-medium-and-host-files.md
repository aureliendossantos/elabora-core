`readMedium { path }` gives the bytes of a binary file of the project with its media type, at the desktop and in a browser, where they are transferred rather than copied: a window draws a line's waveform or plays it from them. A browser's host finds what to copy beside the engine with `pocketCatalogueFiles` (`@elabora/node`, the local voices' catalogue) and `onnxRuntimeWebFiles` (`@elabora/engine`, the models Worker's runtime). The key's page also takes a local mirror of the app, `http://localhost` or `http://127.0.0.1`.
