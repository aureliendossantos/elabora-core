A charter's styles and backdrop variants have names authors read, translated with the course (schemas 0.1.6): `"lead": { "label": "Lead", "description": "Large opening paragraph" }`, where the description alone still names one by its identifier, capitalised. The values of a component's choices read in words in the inspector with `x-enum-labels`, as a free object's do (`{ "stack": "Stacked", "split": "Page on the right" }`), and are translated with the course too; their keys are checked against the enum (`E_COMPONENT_SCHEMA`). The settings a fork adds (`propsExtend`) are translated as its other settings. The seven templates name every style, variant and choice, in English and in French, and their descriptions no longer repeat those names; the examples name theirs.
