A 3D diorama of a canal lock that learners operate (explore), go through step by step in a bubble that points at what moves (explain), or that screen events and dialogue choices drive (display). Its rules are fixed and shared by every mode: a pair of gates only opens when the water is level on both sides, water only flows downhill through the paddles, the chamber only fills once it is closed off, and the boat floats on the water under it; mistakes get a visible reaction (and an explanation for screen readers), never a penalty, and three hints of increasing precision guide learners who are stuck.

Authors change its look and density without touching the rules: visual style (illustrated, playful, technical), camera (isometric, top-down, section), information level, annotations, motion, entrance, materials and the rise between the canals; every word it shows can be reworded in the block.

Placed on several screens as one lock (continuity), it goes from one to the next without reloading: the camera, the water, the gates and the paddles move to what the arriving screen shows, and a boat that is elsewhere there fades out and comes back at its new place, so that it never sails through a gate or a wall.
