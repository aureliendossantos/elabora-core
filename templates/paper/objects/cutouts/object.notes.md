Decoration of the Paper template: a toy theatre of cut paper behind the course, hills laid on one another along the bottom of the window, a sun, a cloud and a few leaves, each piece casting the soft shadow of a sheet lifted above the one under it. It carries no content: it says that the module moves on. At each screen the hills slide sideways like scenery (the near ones further); at each part the pieces are pulled out and new ones slid in, in stop motion, then everything is still. The variants « calm » (lower hills, a clear sky) and « night » (blue paper, a moon and stars) change the scene the same way; with less motion the new scene is there at once.
