Decoration of the Clear template, behind every screen: a rail of hairline segments under the top bar, one per part of the module, and a short accent rule under the part the learner is in. It carries no content and only says where the learner stands; the outline says the same in words.

It moves only when the part changes: it slides to the next or previous part, fades across a longer jump, and is drawn still under reduced motion. It counts the module's sections from the course (one segment each) and runs along the top bar (the safe area).
