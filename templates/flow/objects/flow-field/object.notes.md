# Flow field

**Pedagogical role.** Decoration of the Flow charter, behind every screen: it carries no content. Two bundles of luminous lines undulate low in the window and particles float; at each new screen the particles stretch into streaks and speed up, then settle (the other way when the learner goes back), so the learner feels the module move forward. A new part leaps a little further and shifts the palette from the first colour to the second.

**Editable without code.** The two neon colours and the two ink colours (bound to the theme tokens `color.neon-a`, `color.neon-b`, `color.ink-a`, `color.ink-b`), the field colour (`color.field`; a light field switches to ink), the number of particles, the brightness of the lines, and the strength of the leap. The leap follows the course's direction and the palette the part's rank: no screen or section list to keep.

**Tracking.** None: a decoration has no events and no state. Under reduced motion it shows still frames (dots, no leap).
