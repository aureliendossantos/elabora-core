# D103. The browser engine: a session of the base, on a copy, in @elabora/engine (19 Nov 2026)

A browser engine answers the engine's protocol from a Worker, on a copy of a project of the cloud. It has no folder, no lock, no journal on a disk, no other writer and nothing to watch ([D101](D101.md)). It is made of the base ([D100](D100.md)) and of what the base lacked: the copy as the source of its changes, a browser's storage, a connection to the room.

Decided:
- **It lives in `@elabora/engine`, under `src/browser/`** (`@elabora/engine/browser/<file>`), beside the desktop's session. Its files import the base, `@elabora/core` and `@elabora/format`, never Node: the same checks as the base (a `tsconfig.json` without Node's types, the bundle for a browser of `test/unit/browser-base.test.ts`, which takes this folder too). Its entry for a Worker (`browser/worker.ts`) is what an app bundles. It holds:
  - the session (`browser/session.ts`): the protocol's dispatch over the base's reads, the copy's changes and the services its host gives (D102: none at first);
  - the copy's changes (`browser/copy-changes.ts`), a `SessionChanges` ([D101](D101.md)) over the copy's journal ([D104](D104.md));
  - a store in memory, for tests and the conformance suite under Node, and the browser's (`browser/idb-store.ts`: IndexedDB and OPFS);
  - the room's connection and the media by `fetch`, given by its host (the Worker's) to the base's `SessionCloud`, which does not change.
- **The base's copy of a project (`base/session-cloud.ts`) is reused as it is.** A change made in the browser goes the way a desktop's does: it is appended to the copy's journal, taken in by the session, given to the `SessionCloud`, which records it in its `SyncClient`. The room's changes come back through the host's `write`, journaled as `sync` changes of their author.
- **Its services** start empty (`summary.services: []`): media, voices, word times and packages come in later work. Opening and showing the canvas belong to the window; the engine gives the canvas its renders and the module's files through a message port, and compiles free objects with `@elabora/core/objects/bundle` ([D117](D117.md)).

Weighed:
- **A package of its own** (`@elabora/browser-engine`). Rejected for the reasons of D100: one more package for each checkout and the app's build, for files that are the engine's.
- **A session written from the essay's Worker.** Rejected: it held neither the conformance, nor durable storage, nor the room.

*Revisit if:* the browser engine needs a dependency `@elabora/engine` does not have, or its bundle grows past what a first opening can load.
