# D91. Where a person is, and when a copy ends: the view in the presence, the room's close codes, the folder unlinked (12 Nov 2026)

In a module shared with a team, the app shows who is there and on which screen (Lin 55): an avatar on the screen in the list, and « Dans le projet », where « Mehdi Benali · Plan » says the view of someone on no screen. The protocol carried `screen` and `selection`, but no copy sent them. And a copy whose person left the team, or whose project was deleted, reconnected for ever: the room's close codes 4003 and 4004 (elabora-cloud C37) were not read, and a person removed while the module was closed only met refused connections.

Decided:
- **The copy says where its person is**: `Session.cloudWhere({ screen?, selection?, view? })`. The copy sends it at once, then at most every 150 ms (the last place always goes), and again after each welcome, since a presence lasts as long as its connection. A place unchanged is not sent again.
- **`Presence.view`**, optional: the view a person works in, said apart from the screen, so that a screen id is never a view's name (other copies and the connector read `screen` as a screen). A room that does not relay it yet drops it (elabora-cloud keeps known keys only), and the app then shows the person without a place.
- **The room's close codes end the copy**: 4003 (the membership ended) and 4004 (the project or its team was deleted). Kept out before a welcome, the copy asks the team's list of projects, at most every 30 seconds: `404 no-team` is the end of the membership, a list without the project its deletion; anything else, or no answer, and it keeps trying.
- **A copy that ends unlinks its folder**: no reconnection, the link file removed, and the engine emits `cloudEnded` (`reason`: `removed` or `deleted`). The folder keeps what the copy held, its changes not sent included: it is a module of this computer again.

Weighed:
- **Deleting the folder when the access goes** (stricter for a client's project). Rejected by the founder's choice on Lin 55: the copy stays in « Mes modules », detached, and what was not sent is not lost.
- **The view written in `screen`** (no change of protocol). Rejected: other copies would read a view's name as a screen id.

*Revisit if:* the room sends a role change on open connections (a `role` message, as elabora-cloud's protocol.md says it does), or relays more than these three fields.
