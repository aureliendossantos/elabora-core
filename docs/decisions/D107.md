# D107. Media in a browser: Mediabunny, and AAC only where the browser has its encoder (19 Nov 2026)

A browser reads and writes media with WebCodecs, which neither reads containers nor writes them. Chromium and Firefox under Linux have no AAC encoder.

Decided:
- **Mediabunny** (MPL-2.0, unmodified) reads, rewraps and writes containers in the media Worker ([D106](D106.md)), a dependency of `@elabora/engine` loaded only there: nothing of it in the engine's Worker.
- **The AAC encoder in wasm (`@mediabunny/aac-encoder`, libavcodec's code under the LGPL) is for tests only**: a development dependency of the core's repository, never imported by a published package nor by what the app bundles. The media Worker knows it only by an address given at its opening; the app gives none until the founder has reviewed its licence. Without an encoder, what must encode sound answers `E_MEDIA_ENCODER_MISSING`; the rest (an image, a video whose sound is already AAC or silent, a conformant M4A or MP3) works.
- **No libwebp in wasm**: the canvas encodes WebP (`OffscreenCanvas.convertToBlob`) where it can; where it cannot (Safari), an image becomes JPEG, or PNG when it has transparency, two masters the desktop accepts too. **No ffmpeg.wasm** (GPL, 32 MB, 15 to 60 times slower).

*Revisit if:* the founder accepts the AAC encoder for the product, or a browser reaching authors lacks WebCodecs.
