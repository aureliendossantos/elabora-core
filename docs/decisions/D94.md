# D94. The role in `welcome`: a copy knows it before sending anything (12 Nov 2026)

D93 gave the copy a role only when it changed while the copy was connected. A copy opened as a viewer, or demoted while offline, did not know it: it sent what waited, the room refused it (`E_SYNC_READ_ONLY`), and the change left the module although the person had done nothing wrong. A copy promoted while offline stayed a viewer until it was opened again.

Decided:
- **`welcome` says the person's role**: `{ type: 'welcome', protocol, head, peers, role?: 'owner' | 'editor' | 'viewer' }`, optional. The room (elabora-cloud) fills it from the member's role at the connection.
- **The copy takes it before anything waiting goes.** The role is set as the welcome is read, before the catch-up and the pump that follows it:
  - **a viewer keeps** what waits (opened as one, or demoted while away), as after a `role` message (D93);
  - **an editor or an owner again sends** what it kept.
  - The engine emits `cloudRole` when the role differs from the one the copy had (the app's first word on a module opened as a viewer), and `cloudStatus` with it.
- **A room that leaves it out changes nothing**: the copy's role stays as it was (unknown at first), and it writes as before D94; a viewer's change is refused by that room, as before.

Weighed:
- **The app telling the engine the role it read from the team's list.** Rejected: the list may be older than the connection, and the room is the one that refuses.
- **A `role` message right after `welcome`.** Rejected: the copy may send what waited between the two.

*Revisit if:* a role other than these three appears, or the room learns to keep a viewer's changes for later itself.
