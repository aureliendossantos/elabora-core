# D114. A browser's models run in a Worker of their own, onnxruntime-web loaded by address, the code that reads numbers in the core (20 Nov 2026)

The desktop runs its models on onnxruntime-node in a process of its own (D27). A browser has no process: a French alignment takes 1.6 GB and seconds of computing, which must not hold the engine's Worker, and the engine's Worker must not carry 12 MB of wasm for a module that never times a word.

Decided:
- **A models Worker, apart**, started by the engine's Worker at the first word to time, as the media Worker is (D106): `browser/models/worker.ts`. The engine's open message gives its address (`modelsWorker`) and its runtime's (`ort`, `ortWasm`); without them the session has no `align` service. **One job at a time**: a French aligner and a French voice would not fit together. It keeps the last aligner and the last voice loaded (D127), both when their language's models are light, one only otherwise (French: the other is freed first), says how a line's saying goes (progress) and stops it when asked, makes no request but for its runtime's files and holds no secret: its models come from the origin's private file system (D113). A key never reaches it.
- **onnxruntime-web, of the desktop's onnxruntime-node's version** (1.23.2, the engine's dependency), **served by the app and loaded by address** (`import(ort)`, its wasm from `ortWasm`), never from a CDN and never in a bundle: a test checks that neither the engine's Worker nor the models Worker bundles any of it, nor Mediabunny.
- **Threads**: `min(4, cores / 2)` when the page is cross-origin isolated (C105), one otherwise. On one thread everything runs, slower; nothing is refused for it.
- **The code that reads numbers is the core's** (`@elabora/core/align/`: the Viterbi search, the words a voice says, numbers spelt out, the windows of wav2vec2, the lines to time and their results), **and what loads a model takes two seams**: `OnnxRuntime` (`@elabora/core/util/onnx`, the part of onnxruntime-common the code uses: onnxruntime-node at the desktop, onnxruntime-web here) and the model's files by name (`ModelFile`, `@elabora/core/util/model-files`: the desktop's disk, a browser's OPFS). The desktop's words stay the same to the byte; a browser's are the desktop's within a frame (20 ms, ms rounding aside), checked on English lines in Chromium (`test/e2e/align-browser.test.ts`).
- **The align service of a browser's session** (`browser/models/align.ts`): `aligners` (each aligner, kept here or not, its size) and `alignNarration` (the lines the core's plan gives, each line's audio read as 16 kHz samples by the media Worker, timed in the models Worker, written by `set_narration_words` as the session's author, one change a line, with the aligner's name and the audio's hash). A line's audio made in the session (`lineAudio`, `recording`) is timed at once when its language's aligner is kept, as at the desktop. The runtime's failure to grow its memory stops the run with `E_LOCAL_AI_MEMORY` (D113) and the runtime is loaded again after it.

*Revisit if:* a model must run while another is loaded (two Workers, and their memory), WebGPU makes a model several times faster in the browsers people use, or the desktop leaves onnxruntime-node's version.
