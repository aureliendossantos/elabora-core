# D92. Two warnings: a chart's item brought before its chart appears, a screen in a section of another part of the plan (12 Nov 2026)

An agent trying the tools of Elabora's cloud found two edits nothing reported. It anchored the moment a chart appears after a word that brings one of its items ([D60](D60.md)): the voice brought that item while the chart was hidden, and learners never saw it come. And it moved a copied screen into a section whose screens cover another part of the plan ([D50](D50.md)): a section with a screen of another part no longer belongs to a part, so it stops following the plan when its parts are reordered or renamed, and the author is not told why.

Decided:
- **`W_CHART_ITEM_BEFORE_BLOCK`:** a chart's item whose moment (`at`) falls before the chart appears: at its `timing.in`, else when its own step opens (a layer's charts, at the layer's opening). The project check of the timeline's times says it, on each timeline (the screen's and its layers'), with the item, when the voice brings it and when the chart appears. The fix: anchor the item to a word said once the chart is there, or bring the chart earlier (its `timing.in`, or its step).
- **`W_SCREEN_OUTSIDE_PART`:** a screen that covers one item, of another part than its section's, in a plan with parts. A section's part is the part named as the section, else the part most of its single-item screens cover, when no other is as frequent (a tie says nothing). The plan's check says it, on the section's list in `course.json`, naming the screen, the section, the section's part, the item and the item's part. The fix: move the screen to a section of its item's part, or move the item to the section's part in the plan.
- **Warnings, never errors:** they refuse no change (a project's room refuses only new errors, [D73](D73.md)) and block no release (a release asks for a validated plan and no errors). Neither is raised on the seven example courses.

Weighed:
- **Errors.** Rejected: both describe a course that plays and can be sent, only worse than meant. An author may also move a screen on purpose and fix the plan after.
- **A section's part from the plan's order** (the section of the first screens of each part). Rejected: the course follows the plan by its sections' screens ([D50](D50.md)), and a part named as its section is what the author sees.

*Revisit if:* a chart nested in a stack whose step differs from its own needs the stack's step (the check reads the chart's own step); plans without parts get parts of their own; authors ask to waive the section warning for a screen kept apart on purpose.
