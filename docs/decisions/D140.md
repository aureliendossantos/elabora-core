# D140. A browser's copy opens only for its account and as its project's; the window can read and erase them all (27 Nov 2026)

A browser keeps each copy it opened for good ([D103](D103.md)): its link, its files, its journal and the changes the room has not placed yet, in IndexedDB, and its media in the origin's private file system. Until now, a copy was named by its team and project only, opened from what was kept before the room was asked anything, and never erased. On a computer two people share, the second could read the first one's modules after a sign-out, and a change the first left unsent would go under the second one's session. And the room a copy joins is taken from its kept link: a script of the origin that rewrote that link once would send the copy's changes to another project of the person's team.

Decided:
- **A kept copy is checked before anything of it is used** (`browser/worker.ts`, `keptRefusal`).
  - Its link must name the team and project it is kept under (`E_COPY_NOT_THIS_PROJECT`). This holds offline too.
  - A team's copy (not `offline`) opens only for the account its link names (`person.id`, which the room's seed writes). The window says who is signed in (`account` in the open request), as it last read it, so that a copy opens when the hosting cannot be reached. Without it, the Worker asks `/v1/account` first, and does not open the copy when no one is signed in or the hosting does not answer (`E_COPY_ACCOUNT_UNKNOWN`). Another account's copy answers `E_COPY_OTHER_ACCOUNT`.
  - A refused copy is neither erased nor sent. The window decides: it may erase it, then open again.
  - The window's seed is its own, taken as it is the first time. An example kept on this device (`offline`, person `local`) is never asked for an account.
- **The window reads what this browser keeps** (`browser/idb-store.ts`, `keptCopies`): each copy's team, project and account, and its changes not yet placed by the room (those its link holds as waiting, and those its journal holds that the link has not taken in yet). The copy is not opened, so modules closed earlier count too. This is what a sign-out can say would be lost.
- **The window erases every copy at once** (`forgetCopies`): every database named `elabora-copy-*` (the copies and their media), their folders in the private file system, and the takes recorded in them (`elabora-takes`). The local models (`elabora-models`, [D138](D138.md)) stay: they are no one's.
  - A copy open meanwhile, in this tab or another, ends first. Its database tells it (IndexedDB's `versionchange`), and its Worker says `E_COPY_ERASED`, closes its session (nothing more is sent nor kept), then lets the database go, after three seconds at most.
  - What could not be erased within eight seconds (a page that still holds a database, a file being written) is named in `failed`, and the rest is gone.
- **What the window does at a sign-out** is the app's. It closes its engines (those still sending once left too), says what is unsent and offers to wait, then erases. The engine gives the pieces.

Weighed:
- **The account in the copy's name** (`elabora-copy-<account>.<team>.<project>`). Rejected: the name is not a lock. Any script of the origin reads every database, so a name would not keep the second person out. What a sign-out leaves must be erased.
- **Erase the copy of another account when it is found, and open the room's.** Rejected: that silently loses the first person's unsent changes, should they sign in again. The window can ask first.
- **Check the seed too.** Not needed: the window gives it, in the same message as the project it names.

*Revisit if:* a browser profile holds two accounts at once (two sessions side by side), or the hosting gives a copy a key of its own to encrypt what it keeps.
