# D125. A medium's facts go by its hash, beside the reference, in elabora-sync/1 (19 Nov 2026)

A browser's copy opens a module with its texts and takes a medium only when something needs it ([D103](D103.md)). Validation, the timeline and the media panels need to know what each medium is before that: what the desktop reads from the file with ffprobe (`MediaInfo`). A binary reference said only its hash (`{ op: 'write', file, blob }`), and its size only in a snapshot (`SnapshotFile`).

Decided: **a medium's facts are those of its bytes, so they go by its hash, beside the reference: never in a write operation, never in the journal.** The room of a project keeps them (elabora-cloud C106) and gives them wherever it gives a reference.

- **The facts, `MediaFacts`** (`sync/media.ts`): `MediaInfo` without its size, with a `version`.
  - The size is the room's word, the one it measures: never what a copy says.
  - The conformance verdict (`FileMedia`) is not part of the facts. It depends on the path (a sound of `assets/sounds/`, [D67](D67.md)) and on the core's rules, and its reasons are sentences a copy must not have the server relay. A copy computes it with `fileMedia({ ...facts, size }, path)`.
- **What travels** (every field optional):
  - **from a copy:**
    - `Submission.media`, the facts of the media its operations write;
    - a message `{ type: 'media', media }` for media the project already holds whose facts the room lacks, or holds at a lower version ([D126](D126.md));
  - **from the room:**
    - `welcome.media: true`: it keeps facts;
    - on a snapshot's binary file, `size` and `facts`;
    - beside `ack`, `change` and `catchup`, `media`: a `KnownMedium` (`{ size, facts? }`) by hash for the media their operations write;
    - on `ack`, `mediaDropped`: the hashes whose facts it left out;
    - a message `{ type: 'media', media }` with facts a copy gave since.
- **The protocol stays `elabora-sync/1`.** A copy ignores a field and a message type it does not know (already the rule). A copy sends the `media` message only to a room whose `welcome` says `media`. Every pairing of an older and a newer app or room goes on working, in whichever order they are deployed.
- **Facts that do not hold never make a change refused.** They come with a change; they are not part of it. A room leaves out the facts of a hash that do not pass `readMediaFacts`, and says so in `ack.mediaDropped`. It places the change's operations as if it carried none. The `media` message, which is nothing but facts, may be refused whole.
- **A key a reader does not know, in both directions:**
  - **a copy newer than the room** gives facts of a version the room does not know, or with a key it does not know. The room cannot check them, so it leaves them out (`mediaDropped`) and places the change. Once the room is updated, the copy's next completion gives them again ([D126](D126.md)).
  - **a room newer than the copy** relays facts of a higher version. The copy's reader (`tolerant`) takes their version, checks and keeps the keys it knows, and leaves the others out. It never gives its own lower version over them.
  - **For this to hold, a new version only adds keys.** It never removes one, never changes what one means or the values it may hold.
- **A wrong or outdated fact is mended by a version.**
  - A room keeps, for a hash, the first facts given at each version, and a higher version replaces a lower one (`replacesFacts`). `MEDIA_FACTS_VERSION` moves when a prober's fault is mended, or when ffprobe and the browser's prober must be brought to say the same (a duration, a codec's name). Copies at the new version then replace the facts of before as they pass ([D126](D126.md)).
  - Within a version, the first word stays: two copies that probe a little differently do not trade facts back and forth.
- **The facts are a member's claim, not a truth.** A room takes them from its editors and owners. No right, no limit and no package depends on them: quotas use the room's size, and a build reads the files. A false fact misleads a browser's display and validation on that project, and nothing else; a member who would mislead can replace the medium itself.
- **Bounds** (`readMediaFacts`): every key of the version present, enumerations (kind, HDR), short strings of a bounded alphabet (container, codecs, profile, pixel format), numbers within bounds (duration, dimensions, rotation, frame rate, bitrates, channels, sample rate). Nothing longer or deeper than `MediaInfo`.

Weighed:
- **The facts in the write operation** (the browser engine's first ask). Rejected:
  - the journal would carry them on every write;
  - an operation's inverse and every undo would have to copy the facts of the medium before;
  - the operation's shape would change for every room and app in service.
- **The verdict (`FileMedia`) with the facts.** Rejected, for the reasons above: it is computed where it is shown.
- **The first word for good, with no version.** Rejected: a prober's fault, once mended, could never reach the facts given before it.

*Revisit if:* a version must remove or change a key (then a new protocol, `elabora-sync/2`); or a package must be built from facts rather than files.
