# D136. ElevenLabs in a browser: the core's provider, the key held by a page of its own (26 Nov 2026)

A browser's session had no ElevenLabs ([D127](D127.md)): a key kept in the app's origin can be read by any script of that origin. The author's key now lives in an origin of its own, `keys.elabora.studio`, where the author types it and which alone speaks to ElevenLabs. Revises D127's point on ElevenLabs.

Decided:
- **One provider for every host** (`@elabora/core` `voice/elevenlabs/`): `createElevenLabs(host)`, the desktop's search, lookup, account, estimate, line and dialogue, given the host's key in use, a way to send an operation, its cache, its charges and its voices' rates. The desktop gives it the key it holds and `fetch` (`@elabora/node` `voice/elevenlabs/`); its behaviour, its cache's keys and its tests are unchanged.
- **ElevenLabs is asked by named operations only** (`requests.ts`, `elevenLabsRequest`): `account`, `subscription`, `voices`, `voice`, `sharedVoices`, `models`, `speech`, `dialogue`, `sound`, `preview`, each parameter checked; anything else is refused (`E_KEY_REQUEST`). `preview` takes no key and reads a voice's sample where ElevenLabs keeps it: its public bucket, or its API's preview route (`/v1/voices/<id>/previews/<name>`, its `payload` only, on a host named in `PREVIEW_HOSTS`: `api.elevenlabs.io`, `api.us.elevenlabs.io`, and the EU's and India's data residency, `api.eu.residency` and `api.sg.residency`; since 27 Nov 2026), as the desktop does. A file of the bucket is written in plain characters: no escape, query, fragment or dot segment. The desktop builds its requests with it; the key's page refuses whatever is outside it. A script of the app's origin can thus use the key for what Elabora asks (and spend credits), never read it nor ask another of ElevenLabs' routes.
- **The key's page** (`@elabora/engine` `browser/keys/`: `keeper.ts`, `page.ts`, `index.html`) holds only the key's field; the « Clé ElevenLabs » dialogue around it is the app's, the same as the desktop's. It takes ports only from the app's origins its host writes in `<meta name="elabora-app-origins">` (HTTPS, or `http://localhost` and `http://127.0.0.1` for a local mirror), checks a key with ElevenLabs before it holds it, keeps it in its IndexedDB (encrypted by a key that cannot be read out) only when the author ticks it, and answers with ElevenLabs' answers, scrubbed of the key. It follows no redirect (`redirect: 'error'`: a fetch keeps `xi-api-key` on one to another origin). Of `account` and `subscription` it gives back only the plan and its count (`SUBSCRIPTION_FIELDS`), never the account's ids or its key's preview; an error comes whole. A ceiling of characters per minute was weighed and left out: a module's narration generated at once is tens of thousands of characters in a few minutes, so a ceiling that lets it pass hardly slows a script that spends, which already reaches only what Elabora asks. The window's port also commits, clears, focuses the field and forgets the key.
- **The session reaches the page by a port** the window transfers (`voiceKeyPort`, as `relayPort`). `setVoiceKey` with a key is refused there (`bad-request`): the key is never given to the session; `{ key: null }` forgets it. Without the page, what needs ElevenLabs answers `E_VOICE_KEY_MISSING`, as the desktop without a key.
- **What a browser cannot read:** ElevenLabs does not expose `character-cost` to pages; a generation counts its expected charge (the ledger's share, D36). What was asked once in a session is answered again from memory; across sessions, the takes are kept (D127).
- **Its host** serves the page with a policy of its own: `connect-src` ElevenLabs' API and its voices' samples only, `frame-ancestors` the app's origins, `Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp` and `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: cross-origin` so that an isolated app can frame it; it writes the app's origins in place of `__ELABORA_KEYS_APP_ORIGINS__`. The page's look (`key.css`) and the dialogue around it are the app's. For the samples of a region's API, `connect-src` also names those hosts (`PREVIEW_HOSTS`); without them, those samples are refused by the browser and the others still play. A screenshot by Playwright in WebKit adds a `<style>` to every frame, which this policy refuses (`style-src-elem`, `inline`): a report of the tool, not of the page, which writes no style (checked with a page with no script and no field, 27 Nov 2026).

*Revisit if:* ElevenLabs exposes its charge to pages, a browser must keep generations across sessions, or the key must be usable from a phone without the local voices' models (the voices service is now offered with the models Worker).
