# D136. ElevenLabs in a browser: the core's provider, the key held by a page of its own (26 Nov 2026)

A browser's session had no ElevenLabs ([D127](D127.md)): a key kept in the app's origin can be read by any script of that origin. The author's key now lives in an origin of its own, `keys.elabora.studio`, where the author types it and which alone speaks to ElevenLabs. Revises D127's point on ElevenLabs.

Decided:
- **One provider for every host** (`@elabora/core` `voice/elevenlabs/`): `createElevenLabs(host)`, the desktop's search, lookup, account, estimate, line and dialogue, given the host's key in use, a way to send an operation, its cache, its charges and its voices' rates. The desktop gives it the key it holds and `fetch` (`@elabora/node` `voice/elevenlabs/`); its behaviour, its cache's keys and its tests are unchanged.
- **ElevenLabs is asked by named operations only** (`requests.ts`, `elevenLabsRequest`): `account`, `subscription`, `voices`, `voice`, `sharedVoices`, `models`, `speech`, `dialogue`, `sound`, `preview`, each parameter checked; anything else is refused (`E_KEY_REQUEST`). The desktop builds its requests with it; the key's page refuses whatever is outside it. A script of the app's origin can thus use the key for what Elabora asks (and spend credits), never read it nor ask another of ElevenLabs' routes.
- **The key's page** (`@elabora/engine` `browser/keys/`: `keeper.ts`, `page.ts`, `index.html`) holds only the key's field; the « Clé ElevenLabs » dialogue around it is the app's, the same as the desktop's. It takes ports only from the app's origins its host writes in `<meta name="elabora-app-origins">`, checks a key with ElevenLabs before it holds it, keeps it in its IndexedDB (encrypted by a key that cannot be read out) only when the author ticks it, and answers with ElevenLabs' answers, scrubbed of the key. The window's port also commits, clears, focuses the field and forgets the key.
- **The session reaches the page by a port** the window transfers (`voiceKeyPort`, as `relayPort`). `setVoiceKey` with a key is refused there (`bad-request`): the key is never given to the session; `{ key: null }` forgets it. Without the page, what needs ElevenLabs answers `E_VOICE_KEY_MISSING`, as the desktop without a key.
- **What a browser cannot read:** ElevenLabs does not expose `character-cost` to pages; a generation counts its expected charge (the ledger's share, D36). What was asked once in a session is answered again from memory; across sessions, the takes are kept (D127).
- **Its host** serves the page with a policy of its own: `connect-src` ElevenLabs' API and its voices' samples only, `frame-ancestors` the app's origins, `Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp` and `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: cross-origin` so that an isolated app can frame it; it writes the app's origins in place of `__ELABORA_KEYS_APP_ORIGINS__`. The page's look (`key.css`) and the dialogue around it are the app's.

*Revisit if:* ElevenLabs exposes its charge to pages, a browser must keep generations across sessions, or the key must be usable from a phone without the local voices' models (the voices service is now offered with the models Worker).
