# D137. A module in another module's charter, in a browser: the charter picked by the core (26 Nov 2026)

On a computer, « Nouveau module » can start a module in the charter of another module of the library ([D69](D69.md)): `copyCharter` reads the source's folder, picks the files its charter uses and copies them. A browser has no folder: its modules are a team's projects, whose files its room gives as texts and media by their hash. [D124](D124.md) gave `create_course` a charter's files (`from: { kind: 'charter' }`) for a template's charter, and named this case in its « Revisit if ».

Decided:
- **The core picks a charter's files** (`charterOf`, `@elabora/core/project/charter`), from a course read through five questions: its course.json, whether a path is a file, a text file's text, the files of a folder, the files under a folder. `copyCharter` asks them of a folder; a browser's host of the project's files as the room gives them. Both take the same files: the theme's folder and fonts with their licences, the components, the backdrop's decoration, the player's logo, what those files name, and the generators of the pictures taken with their provenance (`provenanceText`).
- **The browser's host gives what the module starts from**, as for a template's charter: the files picked, texts with their text and media by their hash and size, the source's title and course id for the charter's note, and its theme, presentation and player. `create_course` writes them as the module's first change, checked as anything sent (D124).
- **A source of the same team.** Media pass by their hash: the team holds them already, and nothing is sent again. A source of another team would need its media's bytes moved between teams; the host refuses it.

Weighed:
- **The charter picked by the browser's host on its own.** Rejected: two lists of what a charter is would drift apart.
- **The engine of the source module asked for its files.** Rejected: the source may never have been opened in this browser, and its room already gives every file in one read.

*Revisit if:* a browser makes a module in the charter of a module of another team, or of a module on the author's computer.
