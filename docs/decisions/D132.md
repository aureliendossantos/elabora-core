# D132. In a browser, a screen is measured in a frame kept in the view, and the Lecteur's preview is the canvas page with the player's bars in a frame that may open a link (26 Nov 2026)

The desktop measures a screen (« Déborde à l'écran » of the translation in context) and shows the Lecteur's preview in pages of its own. A browser's window has the canvas page in frames of another origin (D121), and a frame the browser does not draw answers nothing (D130).

Decided:
- **The measure** is `createThumbs(...).measure(screen, language)` (`canvas-thumbs.ts`): a frame of the canvas page at the course's reference size, **in the view, clipped to a pixel** (out of the view, Chromium and WebKit never answer and Firefox takes 96 s), given the engine's `measureRender` (D133: the render with its fields) and asked `shown` then `measure`. One measure at a time; the frame goes after 15 s without one. 150 to 300 ms a measure. The window keeps its `translation-fits.ts`; `host.captures.measure` (`CapturesMeasure`) is the call, and the window's check of the `captures` domain is by method.
- **The Lecteur's preview** is `createPageCanvasHost({ chrome: 'live' })`: the canvas page with `chrome=live` (the player's bars working, `elabora-player-preview/1`) in a frame whose sandbox is `PREVIEW_FRAME_SANDBOX`: the canvas's words, and `allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox` for a link of the course (it opens in a tab of its own, not sandboxed, as from a published package) and `allow-downloads` for a file. The editor's canvas and a miniature never have them (`CANVAS_FRAME_SANDBOX`). An object inside keeps its own list (`pointer-lock`, D119).
- **The Aperçu in the LMS emulator** plays a built package, which a browser cannot build yet: not in this decision.

*Revisit if:* the security review finds a link of the course should not leave the sandbox, or the browser can build a package (then the Aperçu, on the canvas host, by a kind of request of the relay of its own).
