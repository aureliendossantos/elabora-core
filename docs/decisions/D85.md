# D85. A room's refusals are named in the protocol (12 Nov 2026)

A project's room checks a submission before the core places it ([D73](D73.md)): who may write, the paths a project may hold, its limits, the media it must find. The protocol named only the core's four refusals, so the room sent all of its own as `E_SYNC_MALFORMED` (« it is not a change ») with a word of its own beside it. A copy could not tell a viewer's change from a medium that had not arrived yet, and treated both as a change to drop.

Decided:
- **Six codes for the room**, in `sync/protocol.ts` (`RefusalCode`, `REFUSAL_CODES`): `E_SYNC_READ_ONLY` (its author may read the project, not change it), `E_SYNC_PATH` (a file the project may not hold), `E_SYNC_TOO_LARGE` (operations in a change, a text written whole), `E_SYNC_TOO_MANY_FILES`, `E_SYNC_BLOB_MISSING` (a medium the room cannot find), `E_SYNC_ID_USED` (another author's change has its id). `acceptChange` never returns them; `E_SYNC_MALFORMED` keeps its sense: the message is not a change.
- **A missing medium is tried again.** On `E_SYNC_BLOB_MISSING` the copy's `SyncClient` keeps the change, says so (`receive` returns `media`), and gives the same submission again; the engine's session sends the change's media first. After three tries the change ends as any refused change. A missing medium is most often an upload that expired or did not finish, not a wrong change.
- **Every other refusal ends the change** on the copy, as before: it leaves the view, its author is told with the room's message.

Weighed:
- **Keeping `E_SYNC_MALFORMED` with the room's word beside it.** Rejected: the copy reads the code, and the person reads a message that says why; a word beside the code is a second protocol.
- **Trying again on `E_SYNC_READ_ONLY`** (the role may change back). Rejected: a viewer's change would wait without end; the person is told, and the journal keeps the text.

*Revisit if:* a room's limit becomes per team (a plan), and a copy should wait rather than drop.
