# D90. What a copy is told when others' changes meet its own: who replaced a value written here, who kept one an agent wrote over, why a change was refused (12 Nov 2026)

The app says these three things to the right person, once, in their words (Lin 56): « Inès a remplacé votre titre de 1.1 sans avoir vu votre version », « Laissé tel quel : le titre de 1.2, qu'Inès a changé pendant que Claude travaillait », « Votre dernier changement n'a pas été gardé : l'écran 1.2 n'aurait plus de bouton pour continuer ». The protocol carried the facts but not who, nor to whom: a copy was told only of its own changes (`cloudOutcome`), a value left out did not say who had changed it, and a refusal brought a message without its errors.

Decided:
- **The replaced is told, never the replacer** (D73's rule, the founder's choice of 12 Nov). When another's change placed by the room overwrote values whose last writer was a change of this copy (`overwrote[].by`, the submission ids that start with the copy's id), the engine's session emits `cloudReplaced` with that change's author, number and time, and those values as they were (`was`, `unsure` for a file an agent rewrote, D75). Its own changes' outcomes stay `cloudOutcome`; a refusal is said there only (no second `error` event).
- **A value left out names who changed it**: `SkippedOp.by`, the author of the change that last wrote the place since the change's base, set by `rebase` (in the room, and in the copy's own rebase of a waiting change). For the copy's rebase to know it, `SyncClient` keeps the others' changes it took in while changes of its own wait (emptied once none waits), and gives each waiting change those that came after it.
- **A refusal can carry its errors**: `Refusal.diagnostics`, the errors an `E_SYNC_INVALID` change would have added, whole (code, severity, file, path, message, fix), as the commands route already gives them (C50 of elabora-cloud). Optional: a room may leave them out, and the copy then says the refusal without them.

Weighed:
- **The replaced found by person rather than by copy** (any change of the same account). Left for now: the copy knows its own submissions' ids, not the authors of the room's log; the same person on two computers is told on the one that wrote the value.
- **Telling the replacer too** (« Vous avez remplacé le titre de Claire »). Rejected by the founder's choice: only the one who did not see is told.

*Revisit if:* the room stamps a person's id on `by` (then a person is told on every computer), or refusals other than `E_SYNC_INVALID` need words of their own.
