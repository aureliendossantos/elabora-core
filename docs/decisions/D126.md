# D126. A copy that holds a medium gives its facts: with the change that writes it, and in passing for those already there (19 Nov 2026)

[D125](D125.md) puts a medium's facts beside its reference, kept by the room. Media are already in rooms without facts: those that desktop copies sent before, and those that desktop apps not yet updated will go on sending. Decided:

- **The facts are optional everywhere.** A room renders a medium it holds without facts with its size alone.
- **A copy gives the facts of what it writes**, in `Submission.media`, for the media its operations write. Its host knows them, if it does (`CopyStore.mediaFacts`):
  - the desktop's are its probe of the folder's files (ffprobe, the probe cache of `.elabora/`), nothing probed for the occasion;
  - the browser's are its own probe of what it imports (lot 5).
- **A copy completes in passing.** Once a room whose welcome says `media` has told it which media it holds, in a snapshot or beside changes, the copy gives in one `media` message, by batches, the facts it has for those whose facts the room lacks or holds at a lower version. No other copy is asked anything, and nothing is probed for the occasion.
- **A copy keeps what the room told it** (`CopyLink.media`: the size and the facts by hash), so that a browser's copy knows each medium without taking it, across a restart. It updates it from every snapshot, every change, catch-up and `media` message.
- **What a browser's copy sees meanwhile:** the size, always there, and no facts. The medium is "to probe": what needs its facts (the timeline, a validation that reads a duration) takes the medium and probes it, as it would a medium being imported, and then gives the facts it found.
- **Mending** ([D125](D125.md)): a copy whose `MEDIA_FACTS_VERSION` is higher than the version a room holds for a medium gives its own in passing, and they replace the room's.

*Revisit if:* a project's media must be probed for a browser that never takes them (then a service of the cloud probes them, lot 7).
