# D127. A browser's local voices: the desktop's generation in the core, the catalogue served by the app, the takes in the origin's private file system (20 Nov 2026)

A browser's session says lines with the free local voices (D25) on a computer. The desktop's generation (which lines, which voice, a take, what it costs, a take put back) lived in `@elabora/node` with the folder, ffmpeg and the processes it runs on; a browser has none of them.

Decided:
- **What a generation is, is the core's** (`@elabora/core/voice/`): what a provider is and a host's registry of them (`VoiceRegistry`), Pocket TTS's tokenizer, text rules, pins and model (run by the host's `OnnxRuntime`, D114), and `generateNarration`, `estimateNarration`, `setSpeakerVoice`, `voiceSample`, `voiceTakes` given a host (`VoiceHost`: its providers, its writer, where it keeps its takes and how it makes one at another speed, whether a word aligner times a line at once, what it needs first). The desktop keeps its folder, its takes in the project's data, ffmpeg's `atempo` and its processes, and says the same samples for the same seed (checked on an English line before and after).
- **The catalogue is served by the app**, as onnxruntime-web is: `voices.json` and each voice's embeddings (`@elabora/node`'s `voices/pocket-tts/`, 2.5 MB), at the address the open message names (`voices`), each voice checked by its SHA-256. The models come from the models' origin and are kept as D113 says, held from a generation's first line to its last (Web Locks): never removed meanwhile, in any tab.
- **A line is said in the models Worker** (D114), the flow model's state taken as new tensors each frame: onnxruntime-web does not write into tensors given in advance and gives wrong ones back (every take came out failed), where the desktop's onnxruntime-node writes into two sets allocated once.
- **The takes are kept in the origin's private file system**, a folder a copy (`elabora-takes/<copy>/<language>/<screen>/<line>/take-<n>.wav` and `.json`), never in the course nor sent to the room, as the desktop keeps them on the computer. A take is written by `set_narration_audio` as the file beside the command (D108), and timed at once when the align service keeps the aligner of its language. A take at another speed keeps its pitch by WSOLA (`browser/voices/takes.ts`).
- **Refused where a model is**: on a phone (`E_LOCAL_AI_PLATFORM`; the voices are listed, `supported: false`), the French voice on a computer that says it has less than 8 GB (`E_LOCAL_AI_MEMORY`), a private window (`E_LOCAL_AI_STORAGE`).
- **ElevenLabs was not offered in a browser** (`E_VOICE_SERVICE_NOT_HERE`) until the key's own page came: revised by [D136](D136.md), the core's provider with the key held by a page of its own.
- **A stand-in for routine checks**: `voiceStandIn` in the open message puts the core's stand-in in Pocket TTS's place (its voices, said by tones), as `ELABORA_VOICE_FAKE` does at the desktop; never a course's voice.

*Revisit if:* a browser must keep takes across copies of a module (a copy opened again elsewhere has none of them), a voice's take must be the same to the sample on both hosts, or ElevenLabs comes (it came: [D136](D136.md)).
