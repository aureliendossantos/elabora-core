# D104. A copy's journal: its numbers, its history, its undo (19 Nov 2026)

[D101](D101.md) left two points open: how a command and an undo run on a copy, and which numbers its changes carry. On the desktop, a folder linked to a project already answers both: its journal holds every change in the order the folder took it in, its own changes when they are made, the room's changes of others when they come (command `sync`, D83), each with the folder's number; history and undo read that journal with the core's rules (`undoState`, `lastStep`, `redoStep`, `changesToUndo`, `assignBatch`). The window shows a cloud project's history and undoes in it already.

Decided: **a browser's copy keeps a journal of its own, of the same shape, and runs as the desktop does.**
- **A command** is `namedCommand(name).run` through `executeCommand` on the view (the `SyncClient`'s view, which the session's state holds), with the snapshot the session has; its operations and their inverse become a journal entry of the copy (its author, its batch by `assignBatch`, the command and its arguments), written in the browser's storage before anything shows it, then taken in by the session and recorded in the `SyncClient` by the base's `SessionCloud`. A command whose need ([D110](D110.md), `needs`) belongs to a service the session does not have answers `E_NOT_HERE` ([D102](D102.md)).
- **An undo, a redo, an AI batch's undo** are planned on the copy's journal with the core's rules, computed on the view (`undoChanges`), and recorded as an undo entry (`undoes`, with what it left out: D87), as the desktop's `@elabora/node` `edit/history` does on a folder.
- **Numbers.** Each entry has the copy's number, given when it enters the journal: a change made here when it is made, a change of the room's when the copy takes it in. A number never changes: the window's `undo { seq }` and `history` stay valid while a change waits, is placed, or is rebased. The room's number of a placed change is in its entry (`room`), not in the protocol.
- **What history and undo show of a change that waits**: what they show of any change. How many wait is the cloud's status (`cloudStatus.pending`); a change the room refuses leaves the view and is told (`cloudOutcome`), and its entry stays, as on the desktop. The protocol does not change.
- **The room's journal** (`journalOf`, D87) is what the copy turns the room's changes into as it takes them in, and what an agent of the cloud reads; the copy does not ask the room for its whole log. A copy opened from the room's project begins its journal there, as a folder cloned does.
- The copy's journal is kept in the browser's storage and pruned as a folder's is (30 days), what is not yet placed never.

Weighed:
- **The room's numbers for what is placed, provisional ones for what waits.** Rejected: a number that changes as the room places a change breaks `undo { seq }` and the history the window holds; the room's number stays in the entry.
- **Undo from the room's log alone** (`journalOf` of every change since the copy's head). Rejected: the copy's changes that wait are not in it, and a person's last step is often one of them.

*Revisit if:* the window must show which changes wait, one by one, or an agent of the cloud and a browser must undo each other's steps.
