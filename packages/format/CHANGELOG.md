- [79a1804a](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/79a1804a3e8ca9c1bb98d38882e9e8ab2ffc031d) Sounds of the story, ambiences and music (D67). Captions are advised, never imposed. Licences: CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA, own and generated ship; « IA et crédits » gives the credits CC BY asks for, and `CREDITS.txt` lists every sound in the package. Elabora's library « Sons courants » (38 CC0 or public-domain sounds, 11.2 MB, among them Freesound recordings: a wall phone's ring, a call centre, a car passing) and the end-of-dialogue jingle (« WinBanjo », CC0). Agents: `elabora sound search|add|list|generate` (the library, Openverse without an account, ElevenLabs within the quota's threshold), MCP `elabora_sound_search`, `elabora_sound_list`, `elabora_sound_add`, `elabora_sound_generate`, and the skill `elabora-sounds`, named by the course rules (revision 15). phishing-by-numbers rings. The schemas move to 0.1.3 (D64). New codes: E_SOUND_LICENSE, E_SOUND_BACKGROUND, W_SOUND_CREDITS_HIDDEN, W_SOUND_MUSIC_SWITCH_HIDDEN.
- [0b5c974b](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/0b5c974bb00a3a447a317e8d84e8446a35a51d5a) Charts and diagrams (D60): twelve built-in blocks that each say one thing — key-figures, bars, donut, trend, proportion, before-after, levels (funnel or pyramid), process, cycle, matrix, milestones and diagram (Mermaid drawn by Pele) — with a takeaway as their title, a highlighted item and a data table for screen readers. A trend in predict mode lets learners draw the rest of the line before revealing it (unscored by default). Diagrams translate word by word into `labels`. New codes: E_CHART_ITEM_DUPLICATE, E_CHART_ITEM_UNKNOWN, E_CHART_DATA, W_CHART_ORDER, E_DIAGRAM_TYPE, E_DIAGRAM_SYNTAX, W_DIAGRAM_COLOR. Free objects may import `d3` and `@elabora/charts`, the kit the blocks move with; the `elabora-charts` skill and the "Charts and diagrams" page guide agents. `elabora timeline` lists a screen's chart beats and `elabora look --sheet` captures each one once its item has arrived; the LMS emulator answers a trend to predict through its slider. While a chart comes in (its entrance, or the voice still bringing its items), pointing waits: no tooltip and nothing pointed at until it is all there. A step lasts until its last chart item has arrived, so a beat near the end of the voice is never cut short. A funnel can leave out the drop between its rows (`showDrops: false`), and a trend the values written on its line (`valueLabels`: `ends`, `end` or `none`). A new sample, phishing-by-numbers, shows the twelve on narrated slides.

- [90aa3025](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/90aa3025d2ad54d9e901f2f9d52888653b7f386e) Learners play dialogues (D61). The thread is a log whose bubbles arrive whole, read « Marc : … » by screen readers: characters' bubbles with their portrait and name, the learner's, narration, and trainer notes whose quality is said in words. The options come in a room under the thread that keeps its height, and the focus waits in its group « Votre réponse » once the learner answers, never before. Two styles: `messaging`, a chat thread, and `scene`, the backdrop and the cast with the bubbles between them and a History (a thread with portraits on a phone). Lines follow one another at a reading pace or on « Suite », the words lit in ink as the voice says them (or letter by letter, or at once); a line's words, its character's mouth, expression and gestures and its cues follow its audio's own time, and it ends on the audio's end. Speed, volume and « Couper les voix » apply; a refused autoplay leaves « Écouter la réplique » in the bubble. « Choisir autrement », « Reprendre d'ici », « Recommencer », « Réécouter », the endings found and the debrief come as the mode allows. In a slide the dialogue's box keeps its size: nothing moves while it plays. Reduced motion and the new « Affichage » setting « Texte : tout de suite » show lines whole.
  
  The dialogue's progress is saved in the suspend data (field `t`) and counted in its worst case; its conditions, score, completion and the scored run's interactions (one per decision, recorded once) are live, with the `dialogue.*` triggers and the actions `start-dialogue`, `goto-dialogue` and `restart-dialogue`. A screen's transcript has each dialogue's path, and a screen without narration whose dialogue or layer speaks has a transcript too. The theme takes six `dialog.*` tokens (bubbles, text, words said and to be said, notes), checked for contrast once the course sets one. The canvas shows a dialogue after some choices or at a node (`renderScreen` and `canvasView` take `dialogue`), the Review reads the line being said and shows a line (`__elabora.dialogues.current()` and `show()`), and the LMS emulator plays dialogues to their end. Player labels for dialogues come in the seven languages.
