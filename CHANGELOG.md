# Changelog

Version entries are generated from Sampo changesets. Work before versioned releases is preserved in [Development history](docs/development-history.md).

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-27

### Fixed

- [929647e4](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/929647e4e0a44696a4f4697887dca122fc40c90e) `elabora look --block <id> --act …` drives a free object that carries on from another screen (continuity, D65): its frame, in the page's keep layer, is found through the block's `aria-owns`.
  
  Validation and the narration's timeline carry on past a narration line whose text is not a string (a screen that breaks its schema): the line reads as empty, and the schema error says why.
  
  The repository's sample `planetary-gearset` is replaced by `examples/clockwork`, « Why is the hour hand 12 times slower? », in English with a French translation: one 3D object carries its seven screens, from the clock face to the gear train and back to the dial seen through, with the controls of each screen only. The old sample stays as a test fixture of the engine.
- [fae4e279](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/fae4e279d9e4d0c7e6f096e1f57846b345211bbd) A copy keeps its link as soon as it takes in a journal entry it has nothing to send for (a change the room gave it, or one that changed nothing): before, the link it kept named the entry before, so a browser's `keptCopies` counted a change from the room as unsent until the copy wrote again, and a copy opened again applied that change a second time. `keptCopies` counts only the copy's own changes, and `isFromRoom` is `@elabora/core`'s (`sync/copy`).
- [24c1d838](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/24c1d838a275842bca6b1416ed77dbd171afdc62) An agent's activity line that the file watcher missed (FSEvents drops events under heavy disk activity, and Node does not say so) now shows at the session's next rescan, every 15 s by default, and never twice.
- [41a57b89](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/41a57b89e6c6c14e6c546182557a253ca4a7fba1) The narration's seek bar keeps playing or paused as it was: let go at the end of what may play, a playing screen rests there instead of starting over, and a click within a few pixels of the end reaches it. A narration track that a browser decodes a few milliseconds short now ends its screen when it runs out, instead of leaving it playing just before its end (charts with voice beats then stayed spotlit, the pointer off); charts also come to rest whenever the screen's timeline ends.
- [3080b2da](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/3080b2da9e57936b5a1f5394b4e43bf37c659564) `ProtocolClient.close()`: when the connection to the engine goes (a module closed, the engine stopped), every request still waiting is rejected as `closed`, later requests at once, and an answer that comes after is ignored. A transport that cannot send any more rejects the request rather than leaving it waiting.
  
  A session closing (the author back home, another module opened) stops its voice jobs first: the lines said so far stay, and a line being said is no longer paid for and lost when its write meets the closed session. The close version is still taken.
- [990ef79a](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/990ef79a0bc02a26ac5fef68eb070e2e09bb1d50) `elabora object test` says `recorded` for the visual gate when it only recorded baselines on this system, `passed` when it compared, and tells when baselines exist for other engines only (`I_GATE_VISUAL_OTHER_ENGINE`). The contract says how fine a change of shade the 0.1 % tolerance lets through.
- [32eff12e](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/32eff12eae9a511cdd25b638e47269beb313adce) Closing Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge no longer holds a command: once its pages are gone, Elabora waits a second, then ends the browser's process. Chrome 155 on macOS took ten seconds to exit, so each `elabora fit`, `elabora screenshot` and object test paid those seconds when Elabora's own browser was not installed: `fit` drops from 12 s to 3 s, `screenshot` from 11 s to 2.5 s. A look server that is replaced or stopped now exits within two seconds.
- [63f06a98](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/63f06a98363401d493ccaa69fbc88e6c8df5e203) On phones, the lead of the Editorial template's part opener no longer runs under the colour band along the edge of the page.
- [1d742940](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/1d742940676e5111fc8cb36b33b5a0d36f6b0ec5) Removing a glossary entry left its links in the screens, and each one stopped the build (E_GLOSSARY_TERM_UNKNOWN). Writing the glossary without an entry (update_player, elabora player, elabora_player) now removes every link to it in the same undoable change, placed by « Relier les termes aux écrans » or written by hand, inline or by reference: the words stay. The translations that had those texts up to date lose the same links and stay up to date. The result lists the links removed (unlinked) and, per translation, the texts unlinked too.
- [0706f28f](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/0706f28faf115d9cf1c06cddefb2278368f5858b) What the player keeps of a person on the web app's canvas and frames hosts can go when the person signs out: `forgetHostStorage({ origins })` (`@elabora/player/relay-forget`) frames each host's relay page, hidden, and asks it to empty its origin's local and session storage (`relay/forget`), where the player keeps display settings and a preview's progress by module. The relay's kept files stay.
- [990ef79a](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/990ef79a0bc02a26ac5fef68eb070e2e09bb1d50) A generator's assets are stale only when a token it read changes: its provenance records the tokens it read, and a change of another token no longer asks to run it again.
- [e37c52e3](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/e37c52e3f79c2cd09ad632c6b31ce0078b21a0fd) A browser engine forgets a relay port once its page closes the route: the page says `relay/done` on the port, the engine stops what it was answering there and keeps the port no longer, instead of holding every miniature's port until the module closes.
- [e1ae48ac](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/e1ae48ac1573946ed2bf491fbead29296f08afaa) A blank course (`elabora init`, `elabora projects create`, `elabora_projects_create`, the app's blank module) no longer starts with an error: its starter plan has one item, the introduction its screen covers, serving the objective to replace, instead of a practice item no screen covered.
- [990ef79a](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/990ef79a0bc02a26ac5fef68eb070e2e09bb1d50) `elabora <command> --help` prints that command's usage (a group word, its group) rather than every command, and an option error points to `elabora help`. `elabora font add` says the font is added when the course's errors are elsewhere, and how many.
- [7f26bb88](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/7f26bb889294c7104843173540b8b4bb21a6967f) The editor's canvas no longer reports a glide when a block is let go where it rests. Its layout used to give the start of the block's previous glide, so a host's marks went back there and glided again.
- [58bdb944](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/58bdb9446e7d6c0222e5df55cf1ef69b9dca1b51) The canvas host says in the console why its relay answered a request 500 or 503 (an object that did not compile, a route no page holds), not only the status the page sees.
- [b5f1c234](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/b5f1c2344fb546192499a7c2268bec6490876c41) `elabora voice key set` writes the ElevenLabs key whole beside its file, then moves it into place: a crash or a full disk leaves the key kept before.
- [bf891f0c](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/bf891f0cc8a014560d741868f743f50bb3975fc1) The contrast `elabora fit` measures (`W_TEXT_CONTRAST_MEASURED`) no longer depends on the screen's pixel density. With Elabora's browser on a high-density screen (a Retina Mac, Windows at 125 or 150 %), the first window of each page was read at full resolution, because the pixel ratio was learnt from that capture, and the others at half scale: text over fine detail (rhumb lines on a chart, hatching) could then measure up to about 20 % apart from the same screen on a standard screen. The ratio is now learnt once per page from a capture of 100 pixels, and every window is read at half scale.
- [749b9120](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/749b91200d4bf9379a3cf6e15e5d6406c663718c) `elabora fit` no longer reports a line of text cut by one of the player's bars as hard to read: its contrast is measured a few pixels off the bar's edge, which the capture at half scale blends with the bar (a button half under the bottom bar on a phone read 1.79:1 against it).
- [0d189a29](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/0d189a29ff5941be3f253ba232fdaa92693b3ceb) Hashing a project's binary files reuses one buffer instead of taking a megabyte for each file: opening a module of 300 pictures without its hash cache no longer leaves 300 MB to collect.
- [958a9158](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/958a915883ba6c357231e903046709fbe44fb264) A browser's miniature at rest does no work: once its screen is drawn, an animation whose rule wins over the miniature's or started by script is paused, an inline SVG's SMIL animations are paused, and a GIF, an animated PNG or WebP or an SVG that animates shown by an image is shown as one still image (one GIF and one animated SVG in a miniature cost a tenth of a processor core until now). A miniature that does not answer in time is made again after 1 s and 4 s, then its place is freed and it is tried again when its scene is refreshed. A hidden tab draws no miniature: a refresh or a place freed while it is hidden waits for the tab to be seen again, and what failed meanwhile is tried again. A screen renamed changes only its own miniature's version (`thumbRender`), not every screen's.
- [2ec2f31c](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/2ec2f31c15cd893b16e3cc044f2293d1493813bb) Lines said as one ElevenLabs exchange (`voice generate --dialogue`) are cut in the real silence between two voices: silence is measured from the quietest of the gap, not from the voices' level, so a soft sigh or breath that opens a line (« [soupire] Deux minutes… ») stays whole in that line instead of being split with the line before. When only the line before ends on a stage direction (« … [rit] »), the cut is in the last silence, so that line keeps what follows its last word. The sample `diagnostic-reseau` has its two lines cut again from the same answer.

- [c879b612](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/c879b612be0ff312a7e285f654a09924a3e5f143) The dots under a steps carousel stay 24 px wide and tall when the player sets a smaller text size for the window (1280 × 720 and below), so that they keep the touch target WCAG 2.2 asks for.

- [dd8c8ccd](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/dd8c8ccddcef5acc4dd68c24ed7c55c538f6a05f) A component template's `style` takes numbers and tokens only (D141): a number prop, a list's `@index`, `@number` or `@count`, or a token prop whose value is a token's name. - [75c8ed1f](https://github.com/aureliendossantos/elabora-core/commit/75c8ed1f2a2cfc7fef40147f0c166f527d485b9f) With less motion asked, what a course's theme draws in pseudo-elements is stilled too: a backdrop layer's `::after`, a block's `::before`.
